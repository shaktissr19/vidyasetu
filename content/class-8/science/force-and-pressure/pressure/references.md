# Reference Register — Pressure Learning Pack

These references inform academic/pedagogy review only. Learner-facing wording, questions, scripts and activities in this pack are authored as **VidyaSetu Original** and must not copy source text or illustrations.

## Academic/pedagogical references

1. **NCERT — Learning Outcomes at the Elementary Stage, Class VIII Science**  
   https://ncert.nic.in/pdf/publication/otherpublications/tilops101.pdf  
   Review use: pedagogical direction around investigation, observation, interpretation and relating scientific phenomena to causes. This is not used as a source of learner-facing wording.

2. **NIST Guide to the SI — Pressure or Stress (Force Divided by Area)**  
   https://www.nist.gov/pml/special-publication-811/nist-guide-si-appendix-b-conversion-factors/nist-guide-si-appendix-b9  
   Review use: independent verification of the quantity relationship and SI pressure unit.

3. **NIST Guide to the SI — coherent derived units**  
   https://www.nist.gov/pml/special-publication-811/nist-guide-si-chapter-4-two-classes-si-units-and-si-prefixes  
   Review use: confirms pressure/stress uses the pascal (Pa) and that `Pa = N/m²`.

4. **BIPM SI Brochure, 9th edition**  
   https://www.bipm.org/utils/common/pdf/si-brochure/SI-Brochure-9.pdf  
   Review use: SI-unit authority cross-check for pascal and `N/m²`.
