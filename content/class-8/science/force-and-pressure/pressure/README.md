## Learning journey

This pack implements the VidyaSetu method:

**SEE / देखो → UNDERSTAND / समझो → DO / करके सीखो → PRACTISE / अभ्यास → APPLY / जीवन से जोड़ो → REVISE / दोहराओ**

The learner should first notice pressure in ordinary life, then build the idea that the same force has a different effect when it acts over a different area. The formal relation `Pressure = Force / Area` is introduced only after the intuition is clear.

## Scope

### In scope
- pressure as force acting per unit area;
- effect of changing contact area when force is similar;
- qualitative comparison of pressure;
- simple use of `P = F/A`;
- pascal (Pa) as `N/m²`;
- everyday applications: broad school-bag straps, sharp tools, nails/pins, broad tyres/tracks, standing vs lying on soft ground;
- safe observation-based practical.

### Out of scope for this pack
- liquid pressure and pressure with depth;
- atmospheric pressure;
- hydraulic systems;
- advanced numerical problems.

Those should be separate concept packs so mastery can be tracked independently.

## Learning outcomes

After completing the pack, a learner should be able to:

1. distinguish **force** from **pressure** in simple situations;
2. explain that pressure depends on both force and area;
3. predict that, for a similar force, a smaller area produces greater pressure and a larger area produces lower pressure;
4. use `P = F/A` in straightforward problems;
5. identify `Pa` as the SI unit of pressure;
6. explain at least three real-life design choices using pressure;
7. make and record observations from a safe pressure-and-area activity;
8. explain the concept in either English or Hindi without losing the key scientific vocabulary.

## Bilingual terminology lock

| English | Hindi | Learner-friendly cue |
|---|---|---|
| Force | बल | push or pull / धक्का या खिंचाव |
| Pressure | दाब | force concentrated over an area / किसी क्षेत्रफल पर बल का असर |
| Area | क्षेत्रफल | surface over which force acts / वह सतह जिस पर बल लगता है |
| Contact area | संपर्क क्षेत्रफल | touching surface / छूने वाली सतह |
| Pascal | पास्कल | unit of pressure / दाब की इकाई |
| Newton | न्यूटन | unit of force / बल की इकाई |

On first use in Hindi, show important terms bilingually, e.g. **दाब (Pressure)** and **क्षेत्रफल (Area)**.

The pack is intentionally activity- and reasoning-led. This pack follows that pedagogical direction while using entirely original VidyaSetu wording and examples.

The content is **cross-board**. Board-specific curriculum mapping can be attached after academic review; the content itself should not be presented as copied or adapted from any textbook.
