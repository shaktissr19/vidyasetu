## VidyaSetu learning journey

The pack follows:

`SEE → UNDERSTAND → DO → PRACTISE → APPLY → REVISE`

The core learner idea is not memorising a definition. Students should be able to look at a real situation and answer:

1. Who applies the force?
2. Which object receives it?
3. Is it a push or pull?
4. In which direction does it act?
5. Can other forces be acting at the same time?

## Scope boundary

This pack establishes the basic concept of **force as push/pull through interaction**, introduces direction and recognises that several forces may act simultaneously.

Detailed treatment of effects of force, contact/non-contact forces, balanced/unbalanced forces and pressure remain separate concepts so the learner journey stays focused.
