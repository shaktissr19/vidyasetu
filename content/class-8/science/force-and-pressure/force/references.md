## Scientific reference points

The pack uses standard school-level mechanics ideas:

- force is treated as an interaction commonly experienced as a push or pull;
- a force has direction;
- multiple forces may act on one object simultaneously;
- visible motion is not required for a force to be present.

For later lessons that introduce measurement, the SI derived unit of force is the **newton (N)**. The Force v1 learner lesson intentionally does not require numerical force measurement yet; it focuses on conceptual understanding and observation.

Stable SI reference for editorial/SME checking:

- BIPM, *The International System of Units (SI Brochure)* — https://www.bipm.org/en/publications/si-brochure
