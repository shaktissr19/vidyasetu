## Scientific reference principles

For QA, reviewers may use authoritative physics/science references to confirm that:

- contact forces arise through direct physical interaction,
- friction is a contact interaction between surfaces,
- magnetic and electrostatic interactions can act across a gap,
- gravitational interaction does not require direct contact,
- magnetic interactions may produce attraction or repulsion,
- a stationary object may still experience forces.

## Practical verification
