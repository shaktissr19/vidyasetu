# Effects of Force — Media Production Scripts

All media remains DRAFT until academic, Hindi, accessibility and technical QA are complete.

## Asset 1 — Concept video

**Asset ID:** VS-EFFECTS-VIDEO-01  
**Target duration:** 5–6 minutes  
**Visual strategy:** real objects + simple motion arrows + brief presenter/voiceover. Avoid a long talking-head lecture.

### Scene 1 — Hook (0:00–0:30)

Visual montage:
- kick a stationary football,
- apply bicycle brake,
- deflect a rolling ball sideways,
- squeeze a sponge.

English narration:
> The same word — force — appears in all four situations. But look carefully: the result is different each time. So what can a force actually change?

Hindi narration:
> चारों स्थितियों में बल काम कर रहा है, लेकिन परिणाम अलग-अलग हैं। तो बल वास्तव में क्या-क्या बदल सकता है?

On-screen bilingual keywords, one at a time:
`START / शुरू`, `SPEED / चाल`, `DIRECTION / दिशा`, `SHAPE / आकार`.

### Scene 2 — Start and stop motion (0:30–1:40)

Show a toy car at rest. Push it gently.

English:
> A force can change an object from rest to motion.

Hindi:
> बल वस्तु को विराम से गति में ला सकता है।

Then roll the car slowly and stop it gently with a hand.

English:
> A force can also reduce motion and bring an object to rest.

Hindi:
> बल गति कम करके वस्तु को रोक भी सकता है।

### Scene 3 — Change speed (1:40–2:35)

Use the same toy car. First roll slowly, then add a gentle push in the same direction.

Overlay: arrows showing motion direction and applied-force direction.

English:
> When the applied force supports the motion, speed can increase. A force opposing motion can reduce speed.

Hindi:
> गति की दिशा में बल चाल बढ़ा सकता है, जबकि विपरीत दिशा का बल चाल घटा सकता है।

### Scene 4 — Change direction (2:35–3:30)

Roll a soft ball and give it a gentle sideways tap.

English:
> The ball does not need to stop first. A force can turn its path while it is moving.

Hindi:
> दिशा बदलने के लिए गेंद का पहले रुकना जरूरी नहीं है। चलती गेंद पर लगाया गया बल उसका रास्ता बदल सकता है।

### Scene 5 — Change shape (3:30–4:20)

Squeeze a sponge, stretch a rubber band gently, press modelling clay.

English:
> Some effects of force are not about travelling from one place to another. Force can also change shape.

Hindi:
> बल का हर प्रभाव स्थान परिवर्तन नहीं होता। बल वस्तु का आकार भी बदल सकता है।

### Scene 6 — More than one effect (4:20–5:10)

Slow-motion safe shot of a soft ball hitting a surface and rebounding; illustrate slight compression.

English:
> Real interactions can have more than one effect. A ball can change direction and deform briefly at the same time.

Hindi:
> वास्तविक परस्पर क्रिया में एक से अधिक प्रभाव हो सकते हैं। गेंद की दिशा बदल सकती है और वह क्षणभर दब भी सकती है।

### Scene 7 — Check understanding (5:10–5:50)

Show: cyclist applying brakes.

Prompt:
> What changed first: mass, colour, speed or material?

Pause three seconds.

Answer:
> Speed. Braking force reduces the speed before the bicycle stops.

Hindi:
> सबसे पहले चाल बदलती है। ब्रेक का बल साइकिल को रुकने से पहले धीमा करता है।

### Accessibility

- English and Hindi subtitle tracks.
- Separate transcript for each language.
- Do not communicate effects by colour alone; use labels/icons/arrows.
- Describe motion changes in narration, not only with animation.

## Asset 2 — Practical / demonstration video

**Asset ID:** VS-EFFECTS-PRACTICAL-01  
**Safety:** GREEN — learner-safe with light objects and clear space.  
**Target duration:** 4–5 minutes.

### Materials

- small toy car or safe rolling object,
- soft ball,
- sponge or modelling clay,
- smooth table/floor,
- masking tape/chalk markers optional.

### Safety frame

Keep rolling objects away from stairs, glass, faces and electrical equipment. Use gentle pushes only.

### Demonstration A — Start motion

Place toy car at rest. Apply a gentle push.

Record:
- before: at rest,
- after: moving.

Observation prompt:
> What changed?

Expected response: state of motion.

### Demonstration B — Change speed

Roll the car slowly, then push gently in the same direction.

Observation prompt:
> Did the car remain at the same speed?

Expected: speed increased in the demonstration.

### Demonstration C — Stop motion

Roll slowly and gently stop with hand.

Observation vs inference:
- Observation: car stops.
- Inference: applied force changed its motion.

### Demonstration D — Direction

Roll soft ball along a marked line. Tap it sideways.

Observation:
> The new path is different from the original line.

### Demonstration E — Shape

Squeeze sponge or clay.

Ask:
- Did the whole object move across the table?
- Did its shape change?

Key conclusion:
> Motion is not the only possible effect of force.

### Closing table

| Action | Before | After | Main effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Push stationary car | rest | moving | starts motion |
| Push moving car forward | slower | faster | changes speed |
| Hand stops car | moving | rest | stops motion |
| Side tap on ball | one path | new path | changes direction |
| Squeeze sponge | original shape | deformed | changes shape |

Provide bilingual graphic version.

## Asset 3 — Revision audio

**Asset ID:** VS-EFFECTS-AUDIO-01  
**Target duration:** 3 minutes per language.

### English script

> Think of force as an interaction that can produce change. For Class 8, remember four major effects. First, force can start or stop motion. A kick can start a stationary football; a goalkeeper's hands can stop a moving ball. Second, force can change speed. A push in the direction of motion can make an object faster, while a force opposing motion can slow it down. Third, force can change direction. A moving ball can be deflected sideways without first stopping. Fourth, force can change shape. Squeezing a sponge, stretching a rubber band or pressing clay can change shape. Do not make the common mistake that force always makes an object travel. Shape can change even if the object stays in the same place. And remember: one interaction can cause more than one effect. Ask yourself: what was the object doing before the force, what happened after, and what exactly changed?

### Hindi script

> बल को ऐसी परस्पर क्रिया के रूप में सोचें जो परिवर्तन ला सकती है। कक्षा 8 के लिए चार मुख्य प्रभाव याद रखें। पहला, बल गति शुरू या रोक सकता है। किक स्थिर फुटबॉल को चला सकती है और गोलकीपर के हाथ चलती गेंद को रोक सकते हैं। दूसरा, बल चाल बदल सकता है। गति की दिशा में लगाया गया बल चाल बढ़ा सकता है, जबकि विपरीत दिशा का बल चाल कम कर सकता है। तीसरा, बल दिशा बदल सकता है। चलती गेंद को पूरी तरह रोके बिना भी दूसरी दिशा में मोड़ा जा सकता है। चौथा, बल आकार बदल सकता है। स्पंज दबाने, रबर बैंड खींचने या मिट्टी दबाने से आकार बदलता है। यह सामान्य गलती न करें कि बल हमेशा वस्तु को एक जगह से दूसरी जगह ले जाता है। वस्तु वहीं रहते हुए भी उसका आकार बदल सकता है। और याद रखें, एक ही परस्पर क्रिया से एक से अधिक प्रभाव हो सकते हैं। खुद से पूछें: बल लगने से पहले वस्तु क्या कर रही थी, बाद में क्या हुआ और वास्तव में क्या बदला?

## Asset 4 — Application worksheet

**Asset ID:** VS-EFFECTS-WORKSHEET-01

### Part A — Find four effects

Observe home/school/playground and record:

| Situation | Force applied by | Object receiving force | Start/stop | Speed | Direction | Shape |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Learner must provide at least four situations and cover all four main effect categories.

### Part B — More than one effect

Choose one of:
- hitting a moving ball,
- bicycle turning while pedalling,
- soft ball hitting a wall,
- another approved situation.

Write two different effects that can happen in the same interaction.

### Part C — Explain a misconception

Respond to:

> “If a force acts, the object must move across the floor.”

Write a counter-example and explanation.

Hindi equivalent:

> “यदि बल लगे, तो वस्तु को फर्श पर आगे बढ़ना ही चाहिए।”

एक विरोधी उदाहरण और कारण लिखें।
