## Academic verification targets

Before publication, the Science SME should verify:

- air is matter and can exert pressure,
- atmospheric pressure is pressure exerted by surrounding air,
- air pressure acts in all directions at the intended Class 8 explanatory level,
- straw and suction-cup explanations are framed through pressure difference,
- inverted cup/card explanation acknowledges multiple forces and seal quality,
- indoor air also exerts atmospheric pressure,
- syringe/dropper examples are age-appropriate and scientifically accurate,
- no advanced formula or numerical pressure value is required for the concept core.

## Practical verification

Before filming/publishing:

- use a lightweight plastic cup rather than glass,
- use room-temperature water,
- film over a tray/sink,
- confirm card is stiff and slightly larger than opening,
- show both a good seal and a poor-seal comparison,
- use a safe suction cup and non-breakable smooth/rough surfaces,
- do not use flame, boiling water, sharp tools or pressure vessels,
- do not claim the card-under-cup result is caused by atmospheric pressure alone without acknowledging other forces and sealing.
