## Purpose

This concept closes the Class 8 Force & Pressure unit by helping learners recognise that air is matter, the atmosphere exerts pressure, and many everyday “suction” effects are better explained by pressure differences rather than by a mysterious pulling force.

## VidyaSetu learning journey

- **SEE:** why can a card sometimes stay under an inverted water-filled cup?
- **UNDERSTAND:** air surrounds Earth, air exerts pressure, atmospheric pressure acts in all directions, and pressure differences can create familiar effects.
- **DO:** safe plastic-cup/card demonstration + suction-cup observation.
- **PRACTISE:** 12 bilingual questions with reasoning and misconception repair.
- **APPLY:** explain a straw, suction cup, syringe-without-needle and simple sealed-container situations.
- **REVISE:** bilingual recap + mastery check.

## Core misconceptions addressed

1. Air cannot exert pressure because it is invisible.
2. Atmospheric pressure acts only downward.
3. “Suction” is a separate pulling force that comes from empty space.
4. Atmospheric pressure exists only outdoors.
5. The card-under-cup demo works because the card is magically stuck to the water.
