## Purpose

This concept extends the general idea of pressure into liquids. Learners investigate that liquids exert pressure on the surfaces they touch and that, for the same liquid under similar conditions, pressure increases with depth.

## VidyaSetu learning journey

- **SEE:** why does water from a lower hole in a bottle usually travel farther than water from a higher hole?
- **UNDERSTAND:** liquids exert pressure on container walls and base; deeper points experience greater liquid pressure in the same liquid.
- **DO:** supervised pre-punctured-bottle water-stream observation.
- **PRACTISE:** 12 bilingual questions with reasoning.
- **APPLY:** connect the concept to water tanks, dams, divers and storage containers.
- **REVISE:** bilingual recap + mastery check.

## Core misconceptions addressed

1. Liquids push only downward.
2. Liquid pressure is the same everywhere regardless of depth.
3. A wider container automatically means greater pressure at the same depth in the same liquid.
4. A liquid must be moving to exert pressure.
5. The farthest water jet proves only that the lowest hole is largest.
