## Practical verification

Before filming/publishing the bottle investigation:

- confirm holes are similar in diameter,
- confirm all holes are below the initial liquid surface,
- confirm the lower stream travels visibly farther in the selected setup,
- record that water level falls during the demonstration, so comparisons should focus on the initial/early stream behaviour,
- avoid presenting jet distance as a precise direct measurement of pressure,
- verify adult/teacher preparation of holes.
