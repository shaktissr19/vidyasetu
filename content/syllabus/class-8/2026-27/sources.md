## Source policy

VidyaSetu stores an original syllabus index and an original instructional concept decomposition. Official/reference sources verify book identity, current session structure, chapter/unit/project names and curriculum policy. Textbook prose is not copied into VidyaSetu learner content.

## Computational Thinking and Artificial Intelligence

CBSE launched the CT & AI curriculum for Classes III–VIII for session 2026–27 and released Class 8 Student and Teacher Handbooks.

Official sources:

- https://cbseacademic.nic.in/ct-ai.html
- https://cbseacademic.nic.in/web_material/Circulars/2026/18_Circular_2026.pdf
- https://cbseacademic.nic.in/web_material/CurriculumMain27/CTAI_Pri_2026-27.pdf
- https://cbseacademic.nic.in/web_material/CurriculumMain27/CTAI_Pri8SH_2026-27.pdf
- https://cbseacademic.nic.in/web_material/CurriculumMain27/CTAI_Pri8TH_2026-27.pdf

The official Class 8 guidance states that the Computational Thinking section is a companion to the Mathematics book and follows its chapter sequence. The AI section covers:

- AI Project Lifecycle,
- Artificial Intelligence and Its Applications,
- Data and Fairness in AI,
- Ethics and Responsible AI.

Grade 8 time allocation is 100 hours annually:

- 40 hours Advanced CT Skills,
- 20 hours Introductory Concepts of AI,
- 40 hours Interdisciplinary Projects (two projects of 20 hours each).

## Versioning and review rule

1. Chapter/unit titles are factual curriculum identifiers.
2. `topic-index.json` is VidyaSetu's own instructional decomposition and must not be described as verbatim NCERT subheadings.
3. All indexes require subject-SME confirmation before `FINAL` status.
4. If NCERT/CBSE revises a structure, create a new academic-session/version mapping instead of overwriting historical mappings.
5. Reusable VidyaSetu concept IDs should remain stable where the underlying concept remains academically equivalent across boards or sessions.
6. Public Learn resources may remain unmapped when they are non-syllabus learning, while academic public resources may map to these syllabus nodes.
