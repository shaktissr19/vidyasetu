## Source policy

The VidyaSetu syllabus registry is an original structured index. Official/reference sources are used to verify curriculum structure, book identity, chapter/project names and policy requirements. VidyaSetu does not reproduce textbook prose as platform content.

## Primary official/reference sources

### CBSE — Class VI R3 / three-language implementation

CBSE Circular Acad-17/2026 introduced R3 in Class VI for the 2026–27 scheme of studies and requires the language model to remain school-configurable. CBSE follow-up Circular Acad-25/2026 reiterates implementation requirements and states that scheduled-language R3 resources are being made available through CBSE/NCERT.

- https://cbseacademic.nic.in/web_material/Circulars/2026/17_Circular_2026.pdf
- https://cbseacademic.nic.in/web_material/Circulars/2026/25_Circular_2026.pdf

### CBSE — Computational Thinking and Artificial Intelligence

CBSE released a CT & AI curriculum framework for Classes III–VIII for session 2026–27. At the Middle Stage, it advances computational-thinking skills and introduces foundational AI through worksheets, basic theory and interdisciplinary projects, including responsible-use themes such as privacy, bias and fairness.

- https://cbseacademic.nic.in/web_material/CurriculumMain27/CTAI_Pri_2026-27.pdf
- https://cbseacademic.nic.in/web_material/CurriculumMain27/CTAI_Pri6SH_2026-27.pdf

### CBSE / NCERT — Vocational Education

CBSE lists `Kaushal Bodh` as mandatory for Classes VI–VIII from session 2026–27. The Class 6 book contains six projects across three forms of work.

## Chapter-index verification notes

The registry stores chapter/unit titles only as factual curriculum identifiers. Topic and concept labels in `topic-index.json` are **VidyaSetu original instructional decomposition**, not claims about verbatim NCERT subheadings.

Where the NCERT public textbook selector exposes only numbered chapter links, the index was cross-checked against current 2026 edition indexes/e-resource listings. Before the registry is marked `FINAL`, an academic reviewer should perform a complete chapter-by-chapter comparison against the current official NCERT PDFs.

## Versioning rule

Never overwrite a historical syllabus version when a curriculum changes. Create a new session/version registry and map reusable VidyaSetu concepts across versions.

Example:

`CLASS_6 / 2026-27 / SCIENCE / C6-SCI-04`

may later map to another board or academic year without changing the stable concept identity where the underlying concept remains equivalent.
