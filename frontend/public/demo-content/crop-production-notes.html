Irrigation & Crop Production — Notes

  1. Prepare and loosen soil.
  2. Select healthy seeds and sow at correct depth.
  3. Add nutrients using manure/fertilisers.
  4. Irrigate according to crop and season.
  5. Remove weeds before they compete for nutrients.
  6. Harvest mature crop and store it safely.

Modern irrigation methods include sprinkler and drip systems.
