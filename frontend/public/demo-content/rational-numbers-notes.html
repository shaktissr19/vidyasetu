Rational Numbers — Quick Notes

  • Standard form: p/q, q ≠ 0.
  • Closed under addition, subtraction and multiplication.
  • Division is possible except by zero.
  • Additive inverse of p/q is -p/q.
  • Multiplicative inverse is q/p when p ≠ 0.

Revision tip: practise converting integers and terminating decimals into fractions.
